Open Business directory with no assessment selected, then from an assessment with no assessor
name, then from one with an assessor. Create companies (empty, whitespace, duplicate, very long,
Unicode names), divisions (with and without a company selected, duplicates), write and save
company briefs, move driver records between folders and back to unassigned. Close and reopen
the dialog and check everything persisted, and that assessments did not change.
