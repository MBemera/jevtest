Open "Riley Ready" (fully marked) and the partly marked assessment. On 4 Finalise and export,
check the summary and checklist, "Go to next unfinished step" and activating checklist items.
Try signing an incomplete record, then complete one and sign it: try saving an empty signature,
Clear, a single dot, then a real signature. After signing, confirm the record is read-only
everywhere (Prepare, Assess, Evidence, Findings). Export the PDF and manifest (recipient and
purpose prompts, cancelling each prompt, choosing exports/ in the chooser), and check the export
dialog message. Create a correction addendum (empty reason, a real reason) and check how the new
record relates to the original. Also check "Reload saved record" with and without pending edits.
